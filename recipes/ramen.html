Ramen Recipe

Ramen the lazy way.

Description

Tired from a long day but still need to eat. Make this quick ramen recipe the way college kids do.

Ingredients

  • 1 bag of ramen noodles
  • boiling water

Directions

  1. Step 1 - Open the bag of ramen noodles
  2. Step 2- Pour the ramen noodles into a microwave safe bowl, making sure to remove the flavor packet
  3. Step 3- Pour the boiling water into the bowl
  4. Step 4- Depending on how you like your noodles cooked, you may need to microwave the noodles if the boiling water isn't enough
