Ingredients

  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 10 oz can of cream of mushroom soup
  • 1 14.5 ounce canned diced tomoatoes with juice
  • one half cup long grain white rice
  • three quarter cup shredded cheese

Steps

  1. Step 1- Crumble the ground beef into a skillet over medium-high heat. Cook and stir until evenly browned. Drain off grease, and stir in the cream of mushroom soup, tomatoes, and uncooked rice. Cover, and simmer over low heat, stirring occasionally, until rice is tender, about 15 to 20 minutes.
  2. Step 2- Preheat the oven's broiler. When the rice is done cooking, transfer the contents of the skillet into a 9x9-inch casserole dish. Cover with a layer of cheese.
  3. Step 3- Broil until the cheese is melted and toasty. Season with salt and pepper to taste, and enjoy!
